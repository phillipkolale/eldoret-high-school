About

Page 02

Eldoret High School exists to prepare young people not just for exams, but for the choices that come after them.

We run a full KCSE curriculum across the sciences, humanities, languages and technical subjects, and pair it with a co-curricular life that gives every student somewhere to belong — on a field, in a lab, on a stage, or in a club.

Our teachers are the constant: experienced, present, and known to parents by name. Class sizes are kept small enough that no student goes unnoticed.

We are a day and boarding school serving families across Eldoret and the wider Uasin Gishu region.

Mission

To provide a well-rounded education that develops academic excellence, strong character and a sense of service — so that every graduate leaves ready for university, work, or whatever they choose next.

Vision

To be a school that families in Eldoret trust to bring out the best in their children, academically and personally.
